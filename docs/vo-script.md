# Voice-over script: read it, don't perform it

Recorded after the cut. English, calm, a bit dry. One or two lines per beat, then silence while the
screen does the talking. Total speech is about 75 seconds across a 2:50 video, so most of it is quiet.

Rules I used, worth keeping if you rewrite any line:

- Say "Jira", not "the platform". Say "tickets", not "issues" where it sounds more human.
- Numbers out loud: "ten out of ten", not "10/10".
- Never say "zero config". Say "one admin toggle".
- Don't say "AI powered". The judge is one sentence if you want it, and it's honest to leave it out.
- If a line feels long when you read it, cut it in half. Shorter always wins.

## 0:00: the pain

> When something breaks, the first thing I do is search Jira.
>
> And I get this. Forty tickets that all sound like mine. Not one name on any of them.
>
> Who fixed this the last time?

## 0:20: the panel

> This is On-Call Memory. Same ticket, one click, and there are the names.
>
> Three people. Next to each one, the receipts: which ticket, what they did on it, and when.

## 0:40: the receipt

> Click one and you land on the ticket that proves it.
>
> Here's the assignee, and here's the comment that says what they actually did. That's the whole
> claim. A name without a ticket is a guess, so the app doesn't print those.

## 0:55: draft the ask

> Then: draft the ask.
>
> It writes the message for me. Who I'm asking, the ticket I'm on, the ticket that proves the
> pattern, and the fix that worked. Copy it, send it. Nothing gets posted until I say so.

## 1:10: the radar

> It works the other way too. I open a new bug, and by the time I look, the comment is already
> there. The past fixes, who to ask, and the message written out.

## 1:40: the honest empty state

> And when there's nothing to cite, it says so, and tells you what it searched. No invented names.
> I'd rather it be quiet than confident.

## 1:50: the agent (existing clip)

> Same question in Rovo chat. Same names, same ticket keys.

## 2:30: outside Atlassian

> The two tools also come out over MCP, so my own agent asks the same question from a terminal.
>
> One admin toggle first, one time per site. That part isn't magic, and I'm not going to pretend it is.

## 2:50: the close

> Read-only, apart from that one comment. Everything it says comes from Jira itself.
>
> Link's in the description.

## Optional: the one-sentence judge note

Use only if you want one technical line in the middle, and only after the radar beat:

> A small language model decides which old tickets are actually the same problem. If it fails, plain
> keyword ranking answers instead, so the card never goes blank.
